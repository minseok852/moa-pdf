Moa PDF — document conversion software and font sources

The document conversion engines are distributed unmodified. HTTP gzip compression changes
transport encoding only. Our independent worker bootstraps live in public/document-workers/
in https://github.com/minseok852/moa-pdf and are separate from the upstream engine files.

They do not grant rights to documents uploaded by users.
